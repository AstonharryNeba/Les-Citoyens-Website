Welcome

A bilingual home for every learner

Complexe Scolaire Bilingue “Les Citoyens” was built on a simple conviction: that quality education should be accessible to the majority, not the few. Since the project began in 2006 in Nkozoa, the school has grown into a full bilingual complex spanning nursery, primary, general secondary, technical education and teacher training — taught across both the English and French subsystems.

Learn more

Our Mission

To open the door of quality bilingual education to as many families as possible, combining rigorous academics with practical, technical skills and firm discipline — so that every learner leaves Les Citoyens prepared for further study, skilled work, and confident personal life.

Our Vision

To raise learners who can move confidently between languages, between academic and technical paths, and between tradition and change — citizens equipped to contribute meaningfully wherever life takes them.
